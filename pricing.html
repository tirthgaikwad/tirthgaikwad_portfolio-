Questions

Are the prices in rupees, with GST?

Yes, all prices are in Indian rupees. Student and professional plans include 18% GST. Business plans (recruiters, institutions, sponsors) are shown before GST, which is added on the invoice.

How do I pay?

Through Razorpay: UPI, cards, net banking and wallets. Business plans can also be paid by bank transfer after we send a quote.

Does a plan renew by itself?

No. You get a reminder three days before it ends and renew with one click. Nothing is charged without you.

Can I get a refund?

Within 7 days of paying, if you have not used the paid features. Write to us from this page.

I am an admin or a teacher. Do I get premium free?

No. Admin and teacher are roles for verifying students and running College Hub. Paid features are the same price for every account. Only the founder uses everything free.

What happens to my data if I go back to Free?

Nothing is deleted. You keep your teams, reports and chats; only the limits of the free plan apply to new ones.
