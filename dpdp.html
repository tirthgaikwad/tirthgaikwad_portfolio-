Your data rights in India

The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you rights over your personal data and tells us what we must do with it. This page explains, in plain words, how Connect2Minds follows it and where to use each right.

Who we are

Under the Act, Connect2Minds is the Data Fiduciary: we decide why and how your personal data is used. You are the Data Principal: the person the data is about. “Personal data” is anything that identifies you, such as your name, roll number, email, photo, college and the things you post or upload.

Some companies process data for us as Data Processors: our database host (Supabase), payments (Razorpay), browser push services (Google, Mozilla, Apple), and, only when the AI helper is switched on, Anthropic. They may use your data only to do that job for us.

Your consent

  • When you sign up you tick a box to agree to this notice. We record which version you agreed to and when; you can see it on Account & privacy.
  • Extra uses have their own switch, and saying no does not stop you using the site: the weekly email (on Home), push notifications (your browser), cookies beyond the essential ones (Cookie settings) and the AI helper.
  • Withdrawing consent (Section 6(4)) is as easy as giving it: switch an extra use off, or, for the account itself, delete your account. Withdrawing does not undo what was lawfully done before.
  • If we change this notice in a way that matters, we show you a message on the site and ask you to agree again.

Your duties

Section 15 asks you too: give true details (no false name, roll number or ID), do not pretend to be someone else, and do not send false or frivolous complaints. Breaking these duties can lead to a penalty from the Data Protection Board.

Keeping your data safe, and breaches

Passwords are hashed, logins use secure cookies, ID photos are kept outside the public folders and access is limited to the people who need it. If a breach of your personal data happens, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India as the Act and its Rules require, with what happened, what it means for you and what you can do.

How long we keep it

  • Your account data: while your account is open. When you delete it, it is erased after 14 days (logging in before then cancels the deletion).
  • Work other people rely on (messages you sent, team tasks, College Hub posts) stays, shown as “Deleted user” and no longer linked to you.
  • Payment records: kept as long as tax law requires, without your name.
  • Visit counts: 180 days. Privacy-request records: as long as needed to show we answered.

Children

Connect2Minds is made for college students and working professionals. Under the Act, anyone under 18 is a child and needs a parent's or lawful guardian's verifiable consent (Section 9). This is how we do it:

  • Sign-up asks your date of birth. Under 13 cannot sign up.
  • From 13 to 17 you also give a parent's or guardian's email. We send them a link that shows what you share; until they agree, your account is not shown to anyone and you cannot post or message. If they say no, the account and its data are erased at once.
  • For children we never count visits, never track behaviour and never target advertising (we show no advertising to anyone), and recruiters cannot find them.

If you believe a child's data is here without consent, tell the Grievance Officer and we will act on it.

If you are not happy with our answer

First write to our Grievance Officer (Section 13 says this comes first). If you are not satisfied with the reply, or get none within 90 days, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India, set up under the Act, using the process the Board publishes.
