What you are buying

Connect2Minds plans are digital services. Prices are in Indian rupees; personal plans include GST and business plans show GST on the invoice. Payments are taken by Razorpay. You get a receipt on Plans & billing.

Delivery

Nothing is shipped. A plan is switched on in your account as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually within a minute. If it is not on after 30 minutes, write to Support with your payment reference and we will switch it on or refund you in full.

Cancelling

  • Plans do not renew by themselves, so there is nothing to cancel to stop future charges.
  • If you want to stop using a plan early, it simply stays on until its end date and then your account goes back to the free plan. Your data stays.
  • Business plans (Recruiter, Institution, Sponsor) can be cancelled by writing to Support. Seats or credits you have not used are refunded pro rata for full unused months.

When we refund you

  • Within 7 days of paying, if you have not used the paid features (for example: no paid-only filters, extra teams, intros or contact unlocks). Ask from Support with the topic “Payments and plans”.
  • Charged twice, or charged and the plan never came on: refunded in full, at any time.
  • A plan we stop offering before its end: the unused part is refunded pro rata.
  • Accounts closed for breaking the terms are not refunded, except where the law says otherwise.

How refunds are paid

We answer a refund request within 2 working days. An approved refund is sent through Razorpay to the same card, UPI ID or bank account you paid from; banks usually show it in 5–7 working days. We do not refund in cash or to a different account.

Questions

Write to Support, or see Contact us for our email, phone and address. If you are not happy with our answer you can raise it with the Grievance Officer.
