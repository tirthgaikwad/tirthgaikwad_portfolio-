What we keep about you, and why

Connect2Minds helps students and professionals find each other, work in teams and study. This page says in plain words what that needs from you.

What we keep

  • Your account: name, roll number or ID, email (if you give one), password (stored only as a one-way hash), date of birth, college, department, division, city, skills, bio, profession and the pictures you upload.
  • If you are under 18: your parent's or guardian's email and when they agreed (see Children).
  • Account safety: whether your email is confirmed, one-time codes and reset links (kept only as a hash until they run out), and, if you turn on two-step login, its secret (encrypted) and your backup codes (hashed).
  • Reports: reports you send, and reports others send about you, with a copy of what was reported and what we decided.
  • Your ID card photo, if you add one, to check that you really study at your college. It is kept as a private file, never shown on your profile, and only the people who verify accounts for your college and the founder can open it.
  • What you make on the site: messages and files, team chats, tasks and boards, opportunity posts and applications, College Hub posts and answers, notes and papers you share, research reports, your resume, and your My Study timetable, attendance, planner, grades and timer.
  • Activity: who you follow and save, practice answers, notifications, and a history of actions on your account (you can see and clear it under Activity history).
  • Payments: plan, amount, date and receipt number. Card, UPI and bank details go to Razorpay and never reach our servers.
  • Devices: if you switch notifications on, the address your browser gives us for sending them.
  • GitHub: if you add a GitHub username, its public numbers (repositories, followers, stars, languages).

Who can see it

  • People logged in to Connect2Minds can see your profile card: name, picture, college, department, skills, bio, profession and projects. Your email, roll number and ID photo are not shown.
  • Messages are seen only by you and the person you write to; team and college chats by their members (teachers can read their college's class rooms).
  • When you open someone's profile, they get a notification that names you.
  • Recruiters on a paid plan can search verified profiles. They can see your contact details only if you switch on “recruiters may contact me” or list hiring or internships under “Open to”.
  • We do not sell your information or show advertising.

Events

  • When you register for an event, the profile details listed on its form and your answers go to that event's organisers (its owner and managers), after you agree on the form. Volunteers at the door see only your name, college and whether you are checked in. Sponsors get your details only if you tick that box.
  • Your name appears in an event's “who is going” list only if you tick that box. Everyone sees just the count.
  • Your ticket's QR code holds no personal details, only a signed number. We record when and by whom you were checked in.
  • Organisers can download the registration list; every download is recorded. Answers and form details are erased 2 years after the event; we keep only the counts.
  • A certificate shows your name, the event and its host to anyone who has its code. It appears on your profile only if you switch that on.
  • Deleting your account removes your registrations, answers and certificates.

The helper

The round helper button answers questions about the site. Your chat with it is kept only in your own browser (you can clear it), not on our servers. It reads your own data (like your planner or attendance) only to answer you, and it never changes anything unless you press Confirm. When the AI helper is switched on, your questions and the details needed to answer them are sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, to write the answer.

The AI Tutor works the same way: when you ask it something, your question, the subject and syllabus units you picked, the earlier messages of that conversation and, only if you choose one, the file of a shared note are sent to Anthropic to write the answer. Unlike the helper, your tutor conversations are kept on our servers so you can open them again; they are deleted after 90 days without use, and you can delete one yourself at any time. We also count how many questions you ask each day (for the daily limit) and how much each answer cost us, without the content.

Emails and notifications

We may send a weekly summary email. Every email has a link to stop it, and you can switch it off on Home. Push notifications only arrive if you switch them on in your browser, and logging out stops them on that device.

Your choices

  • Download your data: Account & privacy gives you one file with your profile and everything linked to your account.
  • Change or remove things: edit your profile, delete posts, messages, notes, projects and your resume at any time.
  • Delete your account: from Account & privacy. You are logged out and hidden at once; after 14 days your information is erased. Logging in before then cancels it. Messages, posts and work other people rely on stay, shown as “Deleted user”, and payment records stay for tax law without your name.

Your rights under the DPDP Act 2023

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 applies to everything on this page. You agree to this notice when you sign up and can withdraw that consent at any time. You have the right to see what we hold, have it corrected or erased, name a nominee, and have a complaint answered by our Grievance Officer (and then by the Data Protection Board of India). Your data rights (DPDP Act) explains each one and has the request form.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep you logged in. With your OK we also remember your settings on this device and count visits with our own counter (no IP address, not linked to your account). The cookie policy lists every one, and Cookie settings there lets you change your choice.

Keeping it safe

Passwords are hashed, logins use secure cookies, ID photos are kept outside the public folders, and we only collect what the features above need. No system is perfect: if you think your account was misused, change your password and tell us.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes we will update the date above and tell you on the site. Questions go to Support; requests about your personal data to the Grievance Officer.
