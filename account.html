Your account is closed straight away: you are logged out everywhere and nobody can find you. After 14 days everything below is erased for good. Changed your mind? Just log in again before then and your account comes back as it was.

deleteErased

  • Your profile: name, roll number, email, college, skills, bio, pictures and ID card
  • Projects, research reports and your resume
  • Followers, people you follow, saved profiles, blocks and mutes
  • My Study: timetable, attendance, planner, grades, timer and reminders
  • Practice answers, saved notes, notifications and activity history
  • Your opportunity posts and applications, teacher and admin applications
  • Your place in teams (a team with nobody left in it is deleted)

groupStays, shown as “Deleted user”

  • Messages you sent to other people, and team chat messages
  • College Hub posts, questions, answers, events and resources
  • Notes and past papers you shared, and work handed in for a group
  • Attendance and marks a teacher recorded
  • Payment records (the law requires us to keep invoices), with no name attached

These stay because other people rely on them. Nothing in them points back to you once your profile is erased. Want something removed first? Delete it yourself before you close the account.
